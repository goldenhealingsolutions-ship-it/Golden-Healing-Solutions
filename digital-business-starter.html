FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to already have a website?

No. This service is designed for businesses that are starting from scratch or need help establishing their digital presence.

Do you provide my domain name?

Domain registration, hosting, and third-party software costs may be separate from the service fee unless specifically included in your quote.

Do you manage my social media?

The Digital Business Starter Package focuses on setup and guidance. Ongoing social media management can be purchased separately.

Can every business have a Google Business Profile?

Google Business Profile eligibility depends on Google's current requirements. Guidance is provided based on the type of business and its customer interaction model.

Can I purchase additional services later?

Yes. Additional website development, e-commerce, automation, SEO, social media management, maintenance, and other technology services can be added separately.
