📐 What Is a Wireframe?

A wireframe is a visual blueprint of a website or application.

Instead of focusing on colors, images, fonts, or final visual styling, a wireframe focuses on structure, layout, content placement, navigation, and functionality.

Think of a wireframe as the floor plan for your website. It allows you to determine where everything belongs before you begin building the final design.

📋 Wireframe Requirements

Your Capstone wireframe should identify the major layout and navigation elements of your project.

  • Header or branding area.
  • Primary navigation.
  • Main content areas.
  • Calls-to-action or important buttons.
  • Forms or interactive elements when applicable.
  • Images or media placement.
  • Footer.
  • Mobile/responsive considerations.
