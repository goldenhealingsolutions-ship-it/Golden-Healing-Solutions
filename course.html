Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Web Development Academy program

1. Do I need prior coding experience to take this course? +
No! The Web Development course starts from absolute foundations (HTML5 syntax, tags, and document structure) and gradually advances into complex topics like CSS Flexbox, Grid, and JavaScript algorithms.
2. How does the gamification (XP & Levels) system work? +
As you watch training videos, complete quick quizzes, run live code in the sandbox, and pass lab assignments, you accumulate XP points. Reaching XP thresholds unlocks higher Candidate Levels, badges, and Module Boss Battles on your dashboard.
3. How do I earn the official Golden Healing Certificate? +
You must complete all sequential video lessons, pass each lesson quiz with an 80%+ score, submit your capstone lab assignment links, and successfully defeat the Module Boss Battle exams. Once verified, your certificate generates on your profile.
4. Can hiring managers view my course projects? +
Yes! Your completed capstone labs and sandbox projects can be published directly to your candidate profile portfolio, where corporate recruiters on the GH Job Board actively evaluate candidates for hire.
