Privacy Policy

How Golden Healing Solutions LLC collects, uses, delivers, and stores the information you submit through this website.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who We Are

This website is operated by Golden Healing Solutions LLC, an information technology management and business operations firm. For questions about this policy or your information, contact us at info@goldenhealingsolutions.com or 470-313-9473.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the information you enter into our forms. Depending on the form and the options you complete, this may include:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your preferred contact method and your city and state
  • The type of inquiry and the service you are requesting
  • Pickup and destination locations
  • Preferred dates and scheduling preferences
  • Company or organization name and service requirements
  • Packing, loading, and unloading requirements
  • Item or furniture transportation details, including dimensions and special handling notes
  • Any message or additional instructions you choose to provide

We also record basic technical information needed to operate the site securely and to prevent spam, such as the time of submission and a hashed or truncated form of the submitting IP address used for rate limiting.

We do not ask for payment card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information through this website. Payments for confirmed services, if any, are handled separately.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To review and respond to your service inquiry
  • To follow up using the contact method you selected
  • To confirm service availability, scope, scheduling, and pricing with you directly
  • To coordinate a requested service once details are confirmed
  • To protect the website against spam, abuse, and automated submissions
  • To maintain records of inquiries and their status

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. How Inquiries Are Delivered

When you submit a form, the information is sent to our server and delivered by email to info@goldenhealingsolutions.com through a transactional email provider. Where a database is configured, the inquiry is also stored securely so that it can be reviewed, assigned a status, and answered. If you provide your email address, a confirmation message may be sent to you acknowledging that we received your inquiry.

The customer email address you provide is used as the Reply-To address on the notification we receive. It is never used as the sending address, and it is not exposed publicly.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on service providers to operate this website and to deliver inquiries. Depending on the configuration in use, these may include:

  • A website hosting provider that serves the site and runs the form-processing endpoints
  • A transactional email provider that delivers inquiry notifications and acknowledgements
  • A database or storage provider that holds inquiry records
  • Spam-protection and rate-limiting services

These providers process information only as needed to deliver their service to us. We do not authorise them to use your information for their own marketing.

6. How Long We Keep Information

Inquiry records are retained for as long as needed to respond to your request, to coordinate any confirmed service, and to maintain reasonable business records. Records that are no longer needed are deleted or securely disposed of.

7. Security

  • The site is served over HTTPS in production.
  • Form submissions are validated on the server as well as in your browser.
  • Email provider credentials and other API keys are stored as server-side environment variables and are never included in front-end code.
  • Stored inquiries are accessible only to authorised personnel through authenticated access.
  • Submissions are rate limited and include spam-prevention measures.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request a correction, or to request that your inquiry record be deleted, subject to any records we are required to keep. Send these requests to info@goldenhealingsolutions.com or call 470-313-9473.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for adults arranging technology and business services. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services or service providers change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
