Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about our services, monthly plans, and training.

What does Golden Healing Solutions LLC do?

We are an information technology management and business operations firm. We provide IT support, website development and maintenance, administrative and operations support, data and information systems help, workflow automation, logistics administrative support, cybersecurity basics, and professional training.

How do the monthly plans work?

Every plan follows six steps: consultation, service selection, a written agreement, monthly service delivery, a monthly summary, and renewal or adjustment. Plans reserve a defined amount of service time each month.

Do unused hours roll over to the next month?

No — unused hours do not roll over unless your written agreement specifically allows it. Additional work beyond the plan requires approval and may be billed separately.

Are your prices fixed?

Prices on this website are starting rates. Final pricing depends on project scope, service hours, and requirements confirmed in writing before work begins. Third-party fees such as hosting, domains, and software subscriptions are separate unless specifically included.

Do you provide 24/7 support?

No. Our plans provide remote support and administrative management during agreed service windows — not unlimited help desk coverage, 24/7 monitoring, or guaranteed cybersecurity protection.

Is support remote or on-site?

Services are provided remotely unless otherwise agreed.

Do your training courses come with certifications?

Training completion does not automatically provide professional certification, academic credit, or an industry credential unless explicitly stated for a particular program.

How do I get started?

Request a quote or contact us — we will discuss your needs and recommend the right plan or project scope.
